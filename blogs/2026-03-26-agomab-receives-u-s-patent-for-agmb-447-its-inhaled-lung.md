Antwerp, Belgium, March 26, 2026 – Agomab Therapeutics NV (Nasdaq: AGMB) (“’Agomab’”), a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on fibrosis, today announced that the United States Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO) has granted U.S. Patent No. 12,577,230, covering the composition of matter of AGMB-447, to Agomab.
